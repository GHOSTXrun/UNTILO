UNTILO / PRODUCT NOTES

A clear picture
of what’s here.

UNTILO is being built around one outcome: helping new projects prepare a landing page and release materials. The launch studio brings the brief, page, copy, and release visuals into one place.

What works today

  • Enter a project name, description, target audience, and optional HTTPS link.
  • Choose one of three color palettes and optionally upload a raster logo under 3 MB.
  • Preview a responsive landing page generated from a local template.
  • Edit and copy your launch post and introduction thread before downloading.
  • Open your landing page at full size. Download a ZIP containing the HTML page, edited social copy, launch poster, and social cover. Images are included as PNG and editable SVG.

How the studio works

The animated mission diagram is a process playback, with stage progress, role focus, and an activity timeline. It describes the workflow rather than reporting live agent execution. The studio combines your brief with browser-local layouts and copy structures. The four stages organize the work into direction, copy, design, and file preparation. Publishing and social posting are completed separately after you review your files.

The page and downloadable assets are drafts. Review names, descriptions, claims, links, image formats, and social post lengths before publishing.

Your files and brief

Your brief and optional logo are processed in your browser. The text brief is automatically saved in this browser’s local storage for convenience; the logo is kept only in the current tab. Your brief and uploaded logo are not sent to an AI service. Clearing this site’s browser storage removes the saved text brief.

The website loads fonts from Google Fonts and is hosted on GitHub Pages. Those services may receive ordinary web requests. The layout uses system font fallbacks if the web fonts cannot load.

Planned product direction

Future work may connect live strategy, copy, design, and build agents; introduce review and revision flows; and add publishing integrations. These are roadmap ideas, not features available in this release.

Project launch

The official contract address is TBA. The launch link will be published when announced. The launch studio is available without a wallet or token ownership. The site header supports optional Solana connections with Backpack and Phantom. Choose a wallet, approve sharing your public address in that wallet, and open the connected address to view or disconnect it. Mobile browsers can open UNTILO inside the selected wallet using its official browse link. Connecting does not request a payment, signature, or token approval.
